Project Overview

The Outdoor Roomba project, named Rumble, aims to develop an autonomous robot capable of detecting and collecting trash in outdoor environments such as parks, sidewalks, and residential areas. With over 50 billion pieces of litter polluting waterways and roads in the U.S. alone, our team recognized the urgent need for a more scalable and efficient solution than manual trash pickup. Rumble is designed to autonomously navigate complex outdoor terrains, identify common litter items like cans and plastic bottles using machine learning, and safely transport them to onboard storage.

Equipped with cameras, radars, and other sensors, Rumble can detect obstacles such as pedestrians, vehicles, and structures, enabling safe navigation through public spaces. The system leverages GPS for high-level navigation and integrates trash detection models for precision targeting. When the battery runs low, Rumble autonomously returns to a charging dock without human intervention. Additionally, it maintains periodic communication with a remote server for data logging and potential future updates.

Our solution combines hardware engineering, embedded systems, machine learning, and systems integration to address the widespread issue of outdoor litter. While our current focus is on collecting lightweight debris like cans and bottles, the long-term vision is to scale this technology into a fleet of eco-friendly robots that contribute to cleaner, safer communities. We believe that Rumble is more than just a class project—it's a step toward sustainable urban cleanup through automation.
